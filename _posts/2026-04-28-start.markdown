# Evaluation
My first thoughts about artivism/activism were feeling daunted about the task at hand, and so I explored and researched my ideas until very late of the module, I was mostly unsuccessful in my first 3 ideas of artivism namely mental health and isolation, chemical spills into water, and the lone walker, each idea had many sub ideas but were executed in the time given quite poorly so I went back to painting instead of the new medium I was learning – woodcut and improved my results quite quickly, the most unexpected development was how well working bigger in my art work did, and that working larger from a smaller copy or idea, created a guideline to improve my detail work and aesthetic sense on a larger scale. The screen-printing workshop was pivotal in ideas generation as there I found my final idea of overgrowth of manmade structures – how might man made structures be seen once humans are gone, and nature has overridden them!  

I wanted to work with woodcut to have a natural repeat of my work, however I did not have the skill or time to create the work that I had wanted, moving towards watercolour and screen-print I floundered for a while as the medium was not speaking to me – it didn’t lend itself well towards chemical spills, nor mental health, and whilst It did check the boxes of landscapes I struggled to get the texture I required for a good detailed work, I moved towards small egg tempura paintings, which gave me my overgrowth ideas, and bouncing from watercolour studies and egg tempura paintings I bided my time until the formative assessment, which I was told to work bigger, whilst I could work in egg tempura or watercolour larger, I decided oils because it is an easy medium to work large with, I tried a test piece of working with oil on paper, which lost some of it’s perspective but came out fine, and finally did my larger painting in oils on canvas at the end of the day. 

I’ve been really inspired by the post impressionists, the idea of overgrowth or nature outgrowing us was adjacent from the idea of the show that I was watching near the time – Frieren which features a unageing main character coming to terms and celebrating the loss of someone dear to her. In this sense nature is unageing, unlike Frieren, Nature is seen as a force, it is unfeeling and fickle, without foibles it devastates and rewards. To work with nature is what we must learn alike the spiritual we must live one foot in nature, and one foot without it, modern people see too little of the natural and yet are nature themselves.    

The Oil painting workshop gave me impetus into creating the oil paintings of what I have done today, and I would not have the confidence nor the will otherwise to work so large, or be so bold with my work. 

I have looked at Vuillard and enjoyed some of his distempers and oil paintings, these have and will influence my work in the future!
