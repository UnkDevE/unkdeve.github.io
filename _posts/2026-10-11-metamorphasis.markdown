![reads: Painting analysis (header). Oils, too dark; needs definition, maybe brighten, move into more figurative. Watercolour figures better, don't like another design in middle.](/assets/images/METAMORPHOSIS/Doc00010003.jpg)

Here I start talking about what my subject might be: 
I want the background to be nature and the subject to be human. I'm not sure yet whether human comes first before nature or nature before human.

I want to explore the change that comes with merging of the subject object boundaries of a painting, this oil painting I tried that out.
